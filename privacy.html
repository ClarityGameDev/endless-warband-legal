Endless Warband is made by Clarity Game Dev ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, how long we keep it and the choices you have. It applies to the game on CrazyGames, Google Play (Android) and PC.

The Short Version

  • You can play without telling us your real name, e-mail address or where you live.
  • The game gives you a random Player ID. Online features (rankings, mail, chat, clans, the World Boss and redeem codes) keep their data on our server under that ID.
  • We collect gameplay statistics to improve the game. You can switch them off at any time in Options > Privacy.
  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • You can delete your account and all of its data at any time in Options > Privacy > Delete Account & Data.

What We Collect, and Why

  • Your save. Your progress and settings are stored on your device. On CrazyGames they are also kept in the CrazyGames cloud save (in your CrazyGames account, or in your browser if you are not logged in) so you can continue on another device. On other websites a copy is kept in your browser's local storage so progress is not lost when the tab closes.
  • Your online account. When you first use an online feature, or after a few minutes of play, the game creates an account on our server: a random Player ID and a secret key that only your device knows (the server keeps only a scrambled hash of it). We record when the account was created and when it was last active.
  • CrazyGames login. If you are logged in to CrazyGames, your CrazyGames user ID and username are linked to your Player ID (CrazyGames confirms the login to our server), so your account is the same on every device. Your CrazyGames username is then your player name.
  • Player name. The name you pick (or your CrazyGames username) is shown to other players on the rankings, in chat, in clans and on the World Boss board. Names are checked for offensive words.
  • Rankings. Your scores (best wave, dungeon depth, Souls and Gems earned, chests opened, time played) with your name, platform (web, Android or PC) and Premium badge, plus daily and weekly copies for the Today and This Week boards.
  • Progress numbers. Your level, best wave and similar totals are sent when the game checks your mail, so reward mail reaches the right players and impossible scores can be spotted.
  • Mail and codes. Which mail you claimed or deleted and which redeem codes you used, so a reward is never paid twice.
  • Clans and World Boss. Your clan, role, contributions and join requests; your World Boss attacks, damage and daily entries.
  • Chat. Messages you send are stored with your name, clan tag, device type (for example web or Android) and Premium status, and shown to other players. They are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Chat reports. If you report a message, we store the report, a copy of the reported message and your IP address (only to stop one person from abusing reports with many accounts). Reports are deleted after 90 days. To handle reports and abuse we can read a player's chat history, and we can mute or ban accounts.
  • Gameplay statistics. While "Share Anonymous Gameplay Stats" is on (the default), the game sends daily counters: time spent on each screen, which features, heroes and pets are used, where in-game currencies are earned and spent, runs and waves reached, rewarded ads watched, items bought and errors; and once a day your level, best wave, Gem balance, Premium status, login streak, number of heroes, pets and passives, Adventure depth, platform, game version and play time. They are stored under your random Player ID and never include your name, chat messages or e-mail. Switch them off in Options > Privacy: from then on nothing is recorded or sent, and counters not yet sent are thrown away.
  • Technical data. When the game connects to our server, the server sees your device's IP address, like any internet connection. We use it for security: to limit how many new accounts and wrong redeem codes come from one network (these counters are deleted within 1 to 2 days).
  • Your country. Some countries restrict buying random rewards with real money, so the game works out which country you are in: from your IP address (looked up on our server at that moment), and on CrazyGames and Google Play from the country those services report for your account. The country (not the IP address) is stored with your account, and is used only to decide which offers can be sold to you.
  • We do not store your IP address with your account; the only place an IP address is kept is in a chat report you file.

Purchases and Ads

  • Payments are handled by Google Play (Android) or CrazyGames (web). We never see your card or payment details. Your device keeps a record of each purchase so the item is delivered exactly once.
  • Rewarded ads on CrazyGames are shown by CrazyGames and its advertising partners, under CrazyGames' own privacy policy and cookie choices. On Android, rewarded ads (when offered) are shown by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID under Google's privacy policy; you can reset or delete that ID in your Android settings. We only learn whether an ad finished, so we can give you the reward.

Why We May Use It

  • To run the game and the online features you use.
  • For security, fair play, moderation and abuse prevention, and to improve the game with statistics. These are our legitimate interests; you can object, for example by switching statistics off.
  • Where the law asks for consent (for example ad cookies on CrazyGames), CrazyGames or Google asks for it.

Who Else Handles It

  • Supabase, Inc. hosts our database and server functions and handles the data only on our behalf. Data may be stored outside your country; when it leaves the EU or UK it is protected by safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • CrazyGames, when you play there (login, cloud save, ads, payments and chat settings).
  • Google, when you install the game or buy something on Google Play, and for ads on Android.
  • Other players see your player name, scores, clan and chat messages.
  • We do not sell or rent your data. We disclose it only when the law requires it.

How Long We Keep It

  • Account (including the detected country), rankings, clan, mail, code and World Boss data: until you delete your account.
  • Chat messages: 30 days. Chat reports: 90 days.
  • Daily and weekly ranking copies: about 8 days and 5 weeks.
  • IP-based security counters: 1 to 2 days.
  • Gameplay statistics: daily per-player statistics are removed when we clear out old statistics; a short per-player summary (first and last day played, total play time, platform and version) is kept until you delete your account. Totals that are not linked to any player (for example "120 players opened the Shop today") may be kept longer.
  • The save on your device: until you delete it or uninstall the game.

Your Rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict how we use it, and get a copy of it. Most of this is built in: change your name in your Profile, switch statistics off in Options > Privacy, and delete everything in Options > Privacy > Delete Account & Data. For anything else, e-mail Claritygamedev@gmail.com with your Player ID (Options > Data > Player ID). We reply within one month. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Deleting Your Account

  • In the game: Options > Privacy > Delete Account & Data. This deletes your server account and everything linked to it (name, rankings, clan membership, mail, codes, chat messages, World Boss records and statistics) and erases the save on this device and your CrazyGames cloud save. If you lead a clan, it passes to another member. The game then starts as a brand-new player. Purchases are not refunded and cannot be restored.
  • What stays: reports other players filed about your chat messages are kept until their normal 90-day deletion, for safety; reports you filed stay but no longer point to you; and totals that are not linked to any player.
  • Uninstalled the game? E-mail Claritygamedev@gmail.com with your Player ID and we will delete the account for you. Details: how to delete your account.

Children

The game does not ask for a real name, e-mail address, phone number or location, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Chat is optional, and on CrazyGames the game follows CrazyGames' chat setting and turns chat off when CrazyGames asks. If you think a child has shared personal information with us (for example in a chat message or a player name), contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Connections to our server are encrypted (HTTPS), players can reach the database only through checked server functions, and device keys are stored only as hashes. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, the date at the top changes and the game shows the notice again.
