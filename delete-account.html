Delete Your Endless Warband Account

Endless Warband by Clarity Game Dev · Web, Google Play (Android) and PC · Last updated 28/10/2026

You can delete your Endless Warband account and the data linked to it at any time. You do not need to reinstall the game.

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open Options (the gear button) and choose the Privacy tab.
  2. Press Delete Account & Data, read what will be deleted, type DELETE and confirm.
  3. The account is deleted from our server at once, together with the save on that device. The game then starts as a brand-new player.

If you no longer have the game

E-mail Claritygamedev@gmail.com with the subject Delete my account and include your Player ID. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

Where to find your Player ID (while you still have the game): Options > Data > Player ID (press Copy), or your Profile > Copy ID. It looks like 3f6c2a10-8b1e-4c55-9d2e-7a0b5e9c1f42.

No Player ID? Tell us the platform (web / Android / PC), your player name, and roughly when you last played. We may ask a question only the account holder can answer before we delete anything.

What is deleted

  • Your online account and Player ID, and the secret key of every device linked to it.
  • Your player name, rankings (all boards, daily and weekly), clan membership (a clan you lead passes to another member; a clan with no other members is closed), contributions and join requests.
  • Your mail, claimed rewards and redeem-code history.
  • Your chat messages and World Boss records.
  • Your gameplay statistics.
  • In-game deletion also erases the save on that device.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Reports that other players filed about your chat messages: kept until their normal deletion after 90 days, for safety and abuse prevention.
  • Reports you filed about other players: kept for moderation, but no longer linked to you.
  • Totals that are not linked to any player (for example “120 players opened the Shop today”).
  • Purchase records held by the store you bought from (such as Google Play) under its own policies. Purchases are not refunded and cannot be restored after deletion.
